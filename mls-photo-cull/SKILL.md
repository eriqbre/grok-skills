# MLS photo cull

## Login

https://order.raysmp.com/login (Bridget session).

## Tile #1

Must be front of house. Oceanfront water is the only exception. Never aerial / pool / pond as tile 1.

## Taste

- Keep a secondary bath even if toilet visible if it still sells
- Hide primary-bath toilet closeups and empty closets
- Prefer lived-in lanai
- Keep one ground-level rear lawn-to-water shot
- Hide garage clutter, messy bedrooms, amenity dups

## Order of work

1. Site Media cull.
2. Eriq OK.
3. Then Marketing Kit social defaults, or listings email. Not before Eriq OK.

Never Sort Media By Name.

## Listings email

Email `listings@coastalpgi.net` for bulk upload with address, MLS#, Rays link. Cc Bridget and Eriq.
